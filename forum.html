I swear to God, last week while I was herdin' our flock, one of the goats done wandered off toward that old wooden shack. Went chasing after it and found myself right up yonder. The old folks always used to say don't go up there, but Lord, I never paid it no mind, figured it was just old wives' tales. Right now, I'm short three goats.

When I found the place, I didn't go inside, windows was all boarded up thick from the inside anyway. But 'round the house... how do I put it, there was this awful heavy stench, like rotten eggs or a dead carcass. I was 'bout to turn tail and leave when... I heard a sound from inside. Weren't no wind neither. Sounded like somebody chantin' a hymn in there, but the words were all twisted up, like they was saying 'em with some wicked intent.

I swear on my life, I ain't never going up that ridge again. If anybody's headed that way, you best watch your step. That house ain't right.
