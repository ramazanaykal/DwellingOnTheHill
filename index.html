The Descent

A man of faith discovered something beneath the mountain — a wound in the earth that whispered with a voice older than prayer. He built his home directly above it. Not to worship what lay below, but to seal it forever.

To silence the enemy, he studied its language. To break its seals, he read its books. To understand its words, he listened to its whispers.

He listened too long.

Now the house stands empty on the hilltop. The windows are nailed shut — from the inside. Salt lines guard every threshold. Not to keep things out, but to keep something in.

You are Elias. You came looking for a story. The door locked behind you.
There is no exit.

How It Plays

Touch Everything

Pick up, carry, and throw nearly every object in the house. Pull key items into focus and rotate them to read hidden notes.

Open Slowly

Doors don't swing open. You push them with your mouse, feeling the physics and resistance. What's behind them is your problem.

Listen Before You Enter

In the depths, wrong doors mean death. Press close. Listen. If you hear breathing — walk away. The silent door is the safe one.

Trust Nothing

The house watches you. Rooms shift when you're not looking. The warm light behind a keyhole is a trap.

The Book of the Lost

Many have entered. Few are remembered. Leave your mark before the dark takes it.
